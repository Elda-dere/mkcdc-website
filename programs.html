Serving the Whole Person

Programs

MKC-DC works with communities across seven program areas to build human capabilities for a better future.

01

Livelihood and Food Security

Key activities

  • Sustainable agriculture
  • Income-generating activities
  • Self-Help Groups (SHGs)
  • Vocational and business skills development
  • Household economic strengthening
2025 highlights
  • 55,000 participants reached
  • SHGs mobilized ETB 27.7m in savings and ETB 58.2m in loans
02

Child and Youth Development

Key activities

  • Child protection and well-being
  • Youth empowerment
  • Education support
  • Life skills and leadership development
2025 highlights
  • 20,933 children supported
  • 824 graduates, including PhDs, Masters and Medical Doctors
03

Education

Key activities

  • Access to quality education
  • School support and improvement
  • Adult literacy and capacity building
  • Educational resource provision
04

Health and Nutrition

Key activities

  • Community health promotion
  • Maternal and child health
  • Nutrition interventions
  • WASH (Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene) activities
  • HIV/AIDS awareness and prevention
2025 highlights
  • Batu project served 400 mothers and children
  • SHGs mobilized ETB 1.7m in loans
05

Peacebuilding and Social Cohesion

Key activities

  • Conflict prevention and resolution
  • Peace education
  • Community dialogue and reconciliation
  • Social harmony initiatives
2025 highlights
  • 48 conflicts resolved peacefully
06

Environment and Climate Resilience

Key activities

  • Natural resource management
  • Watershed management
  • Environmental conservation
  • Climate-smart agriculture
  • Landscape restoration
07

Humanitarian Relief and Emergency Response

Key activities

  • Food assistance
  • Emergency shelter and non-food items
  • Disaster risk reduction
  • Response to drought, conflict
2025 highlights
  • 35,047 individuals reached in the Oromia food crisis
  • 15,400 children in Tigray received school kits

Strategic Thematic Areas

Food Security

Supporting farmers with conservation agriculture, vermiculture and organic practices.

Education

Expanding access for vulnerable children and life skills training.

Health & Sanitation

Maternal and CYDP projects, sanitation facilities and nutrition programs.

Peace & Justice

Trauma healing, reconciliation and community-led conflict resolution.

Organizational Capacity

Strengthening governance, resource mobilization and digital systems.
