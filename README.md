# MKC-DC Website

Official website for the **Meserete Kristos Church Development Commission (MKC-DC)**.

> *“Serving the Whole Person — Building Human Capabilities for a Better Future.”*

---

## 📖 Overview

MKC-DC is a dynamic, faith-inspired non-governmental humanitarian and development organization headquartered in **Addis Ababa, Ethiopia**. Tracing its roots back to the 1950s within the Anabaptist-Mennonite tradition, MKC-DC facilitates sustainable and inclusive development, builds community resilience, and promotes justice among vulnerable populations to create a prosperous, just, and peaceful society.

---

## 🗂️ Website Structure

## 🌟 Strategic Focus Areas

1. **Livelihood & Food Security:** Conservation agriculture, vermiculture, organic farming, and climate-resilient practices.
2. **Child & Youth Development:** Holistic development, education access, and life skills for vulnerable children.
3. **Education:** Building school infrastructure, providing learning materials, and supporting underprivileged students.
4. **Health & Sanitation:** Maternal and child health, clean water access, WASH facilities, and nutrition interventions.
5. **Peacebuilding & Social Cohesion:** Conflict resolution, trauma healing, and community peace initiatives.
6. **Cross-Cutting (Gender Equity):** Women-led Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and community-driven economic empowerment.
