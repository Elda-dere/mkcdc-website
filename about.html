About Us

Building Human Capabilities for a Better Future.

Who We Are

Meseret Kristos Church Development Commission (MKC-DC) is a dynamic and visionary organization that has been serving the Ethiopian people since 1940. MKC-DC has evolved over the years to adapt to the changing needs and policies of the country. Today, MKC-DC is a legally recognized entity that operates under the principles of serving “The Whole Person” through community development programs. MKC-DC works with communities to improve their lives in various developmental programs such as Child and Youth Development, Education, Health, Livelihood, Environment, Relief and Peacebuilding.

Vision

To see transformed and resilient communities who strive to reach their God-given potential.

Mission Statement

MKC-DC is a Church-based humanitarian organization whose mission is to facilitate sustainable and inclusive development, build resilience and capacities and promote justice among the poor in order to build a prosperous, just and peaceful society.

Core Values

Strategic Thematic Areas

Education
